# Historical-hire calibration

Inspected the eight original DOCX CVs in the supplied `hires/` Drive folder, both job descriptions, the three-page problem statement, six-page answer key, and component map. The session deck was not supplied. The applications folder initially exposes 50 PDF files in its public listing; the case brief describes 60.

The CVs do not contain full interview notes or post-hire outcome narratives. Ratings come from the problem statement: Rohan, Sunita, Aditya, Meghna and Lavanya exceed expectations; Vikram and Rahul meet expectations; Preetham is below expectations. These are eight observational examples across different roles, not proof of causation or a validated predictor of job performance.

Three recurring patterns distinguish the stronger examples: direct operational workflow experience; independently creating practical changes that teams adopt; and taking ownership of difficult problems through closure and learning. Five criteria operationalize these patterns. Each rubric criterion records its specific source hires and rationale. Job descriptions supply role context only and supply no criteria, weights, experience requirements or education filters.

| Criterion                             | PM weight | SPM weight |
| ------------------------------------- | --------: | ---------: |
| Field-level workflow understanding    |        25 |         25 |
| Self-started improvements that stuck  |        25 |         20 |
| Independent ownership through closure |        20 |         30 |
| Learning from uncomfortable decisions |        15 |         15 |
| Operational results beyond output     |        15 |         10 |

Weights are explicit design judgments informed by recurrence, not statistically learned values. SPM raises the independence bar; years of experience, titles, education, location and identity do not contribute to scores. Independent ownership and measurable outcomes also occur in lower-rated hires, so neither is sufficient alone. Failure-learning evidence occurs in fewer profiles and is a weaker hypothesis.

Scores range from 0 to 10 using the anchors stored with each criterion. Missing evidence means 0 with low confidence, not a claim that the candidate lacks the skill. Every nonzero score needs an exact excerpt from the sanitized CV. The application rejects fabricated excerpts and calculates the weighted sum itself. Both role rubrics total 100 and are versioned as v1. Founder review remains required before every communication and final hiring decision.

Historical CVs and original candidate PII are excluded from the repository and public demo. Demo candidates are explicitly synthetic, with synthetic professional content and locally calculated demonstration scores; these are not claimed to be Gemini-generated evaluations.
